# Business Automation Assessment Platform

## 1. Site Vision

A single-page landing site with integrated assessment quiz that helps business owners evaluate their automation readiness. The platform guides users through a 6-question assessment, calculates their automation potential score, and provides personalized next steps based on their results.

**Core User Journey:**
1. Land on page → Read value proposition
2. Click CTA → Fill intake form
3. Complete 6-question assessment quiz
4. View personalized results with score visualization
5. Take action via dynamic CTA based on score
