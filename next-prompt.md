Create a professional 6-question assessment quiz interface for the Business Automation Readiness Assessment platform. This quiz will help business owners evaluate their automation needs.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**

Use Inter font family throughout. Primary brand color is blue (#2563EB). Headings in dark gray (#111827), body text in medium gray (#374151). Clean, generous whitespace. Sections alternate between white and very light gray backgrounds. Content max-width 1200px, centered. Cards have square borders. Buttons are rounded (8px radius). Primary buttons are solid blue with white text, with hover effects (darker blue, slight lift, shadow).

**Page Structure:**

### Quiz Interface

**Header:**
- Simple navigation bar at top
- Progress indicator showing "Question X of 6"
- Progress bar showing completion percentage

**Main Content Area:**
- Centered question container (max-width 800px)
- Large question number and text
- Multiple choice options (4 options per question, radio button style)
- Each option displayed as a large clickable card with hover state
- Options labeled A, B, C, D

**Questions:**

**Question 1:** "How much of your work week is spent on repetitive, operational tasks that don't directly grow your business?"
- A) Less than 10% - I mostly focus on strategic work
- B) 10-25% - Some repetitive tasks but manageable
- C) 25-50% - A significant portion of my time
- D) More than 50% - I'm drowning in operational work

**Question 2:** "How would you describe your current business processes and workflows?"
- A) Fully documented and automated where possible
- B) Documented but mostly manual execution
- C) Some processes exist but inconsistently followed
- D) Mostly ad-hoc, things live in my head

**Question 3:** "What happens to your business operations when you take a day off?"
- A) Everything runs smoothly without me
- B) Minor delays but nothing critical
- C) Some things pile up and wait for my return
- D) Things break down or stop completely

**Question 4:** "If you doubled your customer base tomorrow, how would your operations handle it?"
- A) We'd scale smoothly with current systems
- B) We'd manage with some overtime and stress
- C) We'd struggle and likely drop balls
- D) We'd completely break down

**Question 5:** "How are you currently using automation or AI in your business?"
- A) Extensively - multiple integrated automations
- B) Moderately - a few tools like email automation
- C) Minimally - basic tools only
- D) Not at all - everything is manual

**Question 6:** "How clearly can you identify tasks that would give you the biggest ROI if automated?"
- A) Very clearly - I have a prioritized list
- B) Somewhat - I have ideas but haven't mapped it out
- C) Vaguely - I know automation could help but unsure where
- D) Not at all - I wouldn't know where to start

**Navigation:**
- "Previous" button (disabled on question 1)
- "Next" button (changes to "See Results" on question 6)
- Both buttons use the blue primary color scheme

**Design Requirements:**
- Clean, uncluttered interface
- One question displayed at a time
- Option cards have clear hover states and selection states
- Selected option is highlighted in blue
- Smooth transitions between questions
- Mobile responsive (options stack vertically)
- Professional, trustworthy aesthetic
- Easy to read typography with good contrast
